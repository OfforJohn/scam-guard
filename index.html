More on Scam Guard

Scam Guard is a mobile add-on which helps keep you safe by warning you about scam calls, protecting your identity and securing your device and accounts online. Scam Guard enhances your service by detecting and blocking spam and fraud calls, without blocking your important calls. It's AI-powered, monitoring incoming calls in real-time and notifying you of suspected nuisance calls. Scam Guard is adaptive and self-learning, which means over time it'll get better at determining whether a call is potentially spam or fraudulent.

Scam Guard is only available to EE Mobile pay monthly customers - it's not available to pay as you go (PAYG) customers. Only the main account holder can buy Scam Guard and add it to other lines on the account. This is unless they request it from an EE Guide to give permission to the non account holder to purchase add-ons, either in store or by calling 150.

Please note that the additional lines on the account with Scam Guard added will only receive on-screen call warnings/call labelling. Other features are only available to the account holder.

Turning on Scam Guard for the first line on the account automatically activates the Norton subscription. For security, only the account holder can sign in to the EE Cyber Security app or My Norton using their EE ID. We only share the account holder's contact email with Norton so they can send Dark Web Monitoring alerts. Alerts go to this email unless the account holder updates it in My Norton or the EE Cyber Security app.

Scam Guard is fully integrated into the EE network, meaning we can analyse more information about the call. This increases the effectiveness of detecting scams and number spoofing. Call data is captured in real time, rather than being stored by an app on your handset. This means you're protected from scammers that might be using lots of different numbers in quick succession to try and get through to you.

Scam Guard is a 30-day rolling contract. To cancel Scam Guard, log in to your account, go to the mobile add-ons page, select Scam Guard from your list of current active add-ons and click remove. We'll remove the Scam Guard add-on on your next billing cycle. It can also be cancelled at any time by calling us on 150 or by texting STOP SCAMGUARD to 150. You can cancel via an agent immediately, on your next bill cycle, or on any date in the future. You'll receive a credit for any unused days on your next bill. To add Scam Guard again, text SCAMGUARD to 150.
